使用範圍

目前支援特定格式的華航機組員班表截圖。辨識結果可能有誤，請逐項核對。CrewScheduler 是獨立開發的班表整理工具，非中華航空官方產品，亦不代表公司提供勤務指示；勤務資訊請以航空公司正式班表為準。

Privacy & support

CrewScheduler converts selected China Airlines crew roster screenshots into an editable monthly calendar. Recognition and parsing run on your device. Visit the English privacy policy to understand local storage, sharing, and optional diagnostic reports, or the English support page for help.

This independently developed app is not an official China Airlines product. Verify recognition results against your airline’s official roster.
