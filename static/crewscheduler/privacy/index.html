隱私政策

本機處理為預設。只有當你主動分享或寄送資料時，選定的內容才會透過你選擇的服務離開裝置。

班表截圖、辨識文字、備份及回報附件可能含姓名、員工編號、航班與勤務資訊。請只處理你有權使用的資料，並在分享前檢查內容。診斷回報不會自動遮蔽個人資料。

1. 適用範圍與聯絡方式

本政策適用於由 Swark 開發的 CrewScheduler iOS App，以及 swarkdoit.com/crewscheduler/ 下的產品、隱私與支援頁面；不包含其他部落格、留言或電子報服務。政策說明 App 的一般使用、手動分享，以及開發者收到客服或診斷回報時的資料處理方式。

資料與隱私聯絡窗口：CrewScheduler 開發者（Swark）。如有問題、希望查詢或刪除已寄給開發者的資料，請寄至 swark.yang@swarkdoit.com。

2. 本機處理與儲存

App 使用 iOS 系統照片選取器，只取得你選取的圖片，不要求存取整個照片圖庫。OCR 辨識與班表解析在裝置上執行，App 不會為了辨識而將截圖或文字上傳至開發者或第三方 AI 服務。

  • 你選取的圖片與辨識文字：用於解析及檢查班表。正式發行版本的班表還原檔不保留完整 OCR 原文；開發用 Debug 版本可能保留原文供除錯。
  • 結構化班表與修改：班表日期、勤務、航段、輪值、標籤、必休標記及色彩等，儲存在 App 的本機資料區，以便重開後還原。
  • 偏好設定：引導完成狀態、顯示選項等儲存在裝置上。
  • 診斷記錄：App 在目前執行階段的記憶體中保留最多 2,000 筆近期記錄，可能含班表解析與操作細節；系統也可能依 iOS 規則保存裝置端記錄。App 不會自動將這些記錄傳送給開發者。

App 目前沒有使用者帳號、開發者提供的雲端同步、廣告 SDK、跨 App 追蹤或第三方行為分析 SDK。裝置備份（例如依你的設定進行的 iCloud 或電腦備份）仍可能包含 App 的本機資料，這與 App 自行提供雲端同步不同。

3. 何時資料會離開裝置

班表分享與備份

當你選擇匯出 JSON、分享班表圖片或使用系統分享功能，選定的內容會交給你選擇的 App、服務或收件人。匯出的班表可能含勤務日期、航班、輪值與個人註記；收件人可另行保存或轉寄。未經你主動操作，App 不會將班表傳送給開發者。

客服與診斷回報

一般客服郵件可能包含你的寄件信箱、郵件名稱、描述與自行附加的檔案。App 內診斷回報會在你點擊建立／寄送操作後產生 ZIP；附件選項會依問題類型預選，你可以在建立前調整。

  • 回報基本資訊：回報 ID、時間、問題類型、你的說明、App 版本、Build、Bundle ID、裝置類型、iOS 版本、語系、時區、各類資料筆數及所選附件清單。這些基本資訊會包含在建立的回報中；即使不附 OCR 原文，也可能包含其字元數。
  • 可選附件：原始班表截圖、結果截圖、OCR 文字、解析後班表 JSON 及 App 記錄。只有你啟用且當時有資料的項目才會附加。

請檢查附件選項，並在選取圖片或撰寫說明前移除不必要的姓名、員工編號及他人資料。App 沒有自動匿名化或遮蔽回報內容。你可以取消寄送；如果使用「分享回報」，請確認接收對象。

寄送至客服信箱的內容會由開發者用於回覆、重現問題、修正錯誤與改善相關功能。客服郵件中的功能需求與建議也可能用於功能評估與規劃；寄件名稱與信箱僅用於聯絡，不用於使用者分群。截圖、OCR、班表 JSON 與記錄等診斷附件僅用於相關問題的排查與修正，不另作使用者行為分析。收到的郵件可與寄件者連結，並非匿名資料。我們不會將客服資料用於行銷、出售回報內容或用於廣告追蹤。郵件或分享服務可能依其規範保存、處理資料，伺服器也可能位於你所在國家／地區之外。

4. 資料保留與刪除

  • 本機班表與偏好：保留在 App 資料區供後續使用。可透過 iOS「設定 → 一般 → iPhone／iPad 儲存空間 → CrewScheduler → 刪除 App」移除本機 App 資料；「卸載 App」會保留資料，與刪除不同。刪除 App 不會刪除照片圖庫中的原始圖片。
  • 匯出、分享與系統備份：已存入「檔案」、照片、其他 App、收件人或系統備份的副本不會隨 App 刪除，請到各保存位置另行管理。重新從備份還原可能帶回 App 資料。
  • 暫存診斷 ZIP：App 會在寄送／分享流程結束或捨棄回報時嘗試清除本機暫存檔，並在開啟回報頁面或準備回報時清理超過 24 小時的過期 ZIP；不是背景定時刪除。這些 ZIP 被標記為不納入裝置備份。清除暫存不會撤回已寄出或分享的副本。
  • 開發者收到的客服內容：僅在處理問題、必要後續聯繫與維護相關功能所需期間保留，完成用途後刪除不再需要的可識別資料。你可來信要求刪除並提供原寄件信箱、寄送日期或回報 ID；若有依法必須保留的例外，會在回覆中說明。郵件服務的備份與收件人另存副本受各服務政策影響，無法承諾同步即時刪除。

5. 第三方、網站與資料保護

App 使用 Apple 提供的系統照片選取、Vision 辨識、Mail 及分享介面。你選擇的郵件、分享、儲存或裝置備份服務，依各自的隱私政策處理其取得的內容；我們不會為一般班表辨識接入遠端 AI 或分析服務。

上述 /crewscheduler/ 頁面僅提供靜態產品說明，不含客服上傳表單、廣告、分析腳本或由我們設定的追蹤 Cookie。網站託管與網路服務供應商仍可能為傳遞網頁、維運與安全處理 IP 位址、瀏覽器資訊、請求路徑與時間等標準連線資料；不能將 App 的本機處理原則理解為網站完全沒有連線記錄。

我們採取限制資料用途及存取、避免不必要收集等措施。App 的暫存診斷檔使用 iOS 檔案保護；裝置、郵件與備份的安全也受你的裝置鎖定及所選服務設定影響，任何傳輸或儲存方式都無法保證絕對安全。

6. 你的選擇與政策更新

你可以不選取照片、不建立回報、關閉附件選項，或取消寄送。停止後續分享不會自動撤回已寄出的內容。若要查詢、更正或刪除開發者持有的客服資料，請使用上述聯絡信箱；我們可能要求確認原寄件信箱或回報資訊，以避免向他人揭露你的資料。

App 面向機組員的班表整理需求，不以兒童為服務對象；請勿透過客服提供不必要的兒童或第三人資料。

功能或資料處理方式變動時，我們會更新本頁日期與政策內容；如有重大變更，會透過網站或 App 適當告知，涉及依法需同意的新用途時會另行取得同意。繁體中文與英文版本若有歧義，請聯絡我們確認。

Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: October 28, 2026 · Version 1.1

Roster images, recognized text, exports, and report attachments may contain names, employee IDs, flights, and duty information. Review everything before sharing. Diagnostic reports are not automatically anonymized or redacted.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the CrewScheduler iOS app, developed by Swark, and the product, privacy, and support pages under swarkdoit.com/crewscheduler/. It does not cover other blog pages, comments, or newsletter services. It covers ordinary app use, user-initiated sharing, and support or diagnostic reports received by the developer. Contact the CrewScheduler developer (Swark) at swark.yang@swarkdoit.com for privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent us.

2. On-device processing and storage

The app uses the iOS system photo picker to receive only the images you select; it does not require full photo-library access. Vision OCR and roster parsing run on your device. Images and recognized text are not uploaded to the developer or a third-party AI service for recognition.

  • Selected images and recognized text are used to parse and review your roster. Release versions do not retain complete OCR text in the saved roster-restoration snapshot. Development Debug builds may retain it for debugging.
  • Structured rosters and edits, including dates, duties, flight legs, rotations, tags, personal rest markers, and colors, are stored in the app’s local data area for restoration after relaunch.
  • Preferences, such as onboarding completion and display settings, are stored locally.
  • Diagnostic logs: the app keeps up to 2,000 recent entries in memory during the current process. These may contain roster parsing or operation details. iOS may also retain device-side system logs under its own rules. The app does not automatically send logs to the developer.

The current app has no user accounts, developer-operated cloud sync, advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. Device backups, including iCloud or computer backups depending on your settings, may still include local app data; these are separate from app-provided cloud sync.

3. Sharing and optional diagnostic reports

When you export roster JSON, share a calendar image, or use the system share sheet, the selected content is handed to the app, service, or recipient you choose. Exports may include duty dates, flights, rotations, and personal notes. Recipients can save or forward copies. The app does not send rosters to the developer without your action.

Support emails can include your sender address, email display name, description, and files you attach. In-app diagnostic ZIPs are generated only after you trigger the report action. Attachment defaults depend on the issue type; review and change them before creating a report.

  • Report metadata included in a generated report: report ID, creation time, issue type, your description, app version, build, bundle identifier, device type, iOS version, locale, time zone, data counts, and the selected attachment list. Counts may include recognized-text character counts even when raw OCR text is excluded.
  • Optional attachments: source roster images, a result image, OCR text, parsed roster JSON, and app logs. Only enabled items with available data are attached.

Remove unnecessary names, employee IDs, and other people’s data before selecting images or writing your description. The app does not automatically redact report contents. You can cancel sending; when sharing a report, check its recipient.

The developer uses received support content to respond, reproduce issues, fix bugs, and improve the relevant functionality. Feature requests and suggestions in support emails may also inform feature evaluation and planning. Sender names and addresses are used only for contact, not user segmentation. Diagnostic attachments such as images, OCR, roster JSON, and logs are used only to investigate and fix the related issue, not for separate behavioral analytics. Received email can be linked to its sender and is not anonymous. We do not use support data for marketing, sell report contents, or use them for advertising tracking. Mail and sharing providers may retain or process copies under their policies, including on servers outside your country or region.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Local rosters and preferences remain in the app’s data area. To remove local app data, use iOS Settings → General → iPhone / iPad Storage → CrewScheduler → Delete App. Offload App retains data. Deleting the app does not remove your source photos.
  • Exports, shared copies, and device backups must be managed in their respective apps, recipient storage, or backup services. They are not deleted when you remove this app. Restoring a backup can restore app data.
  • Temporary report ZIPs: the app attempts to remove them after delivery or sharing ends, or when a report is discarded. When you open the report screen or prepare a report, it also cleans up ZIPs older than 24 hours; this is not a timed background deletion guarantee. These ZIPs are marked as excluded from device backups. Cleanup does not retract copies already sent or shared.
  • Support content received by the developer is retained only as needed to resolve the issue, perform necessary follow-up, and maintain the relevant functionality. Identifiable data that is no longer needed is deleted. Email us with the original sender address, date, or report ID to request deletion. We will explain any legally required retention exception. Provider backups and recipients’ copies follow their own policies and may not be removed immediately or simultaneously.

5. Providers, website, and security

The app uses Apple’s system photo picker, Vision, Mail, and sharing interfaces. Mail, sharing, storage, and device-backup services you choose process the content they receive under their own policies. Ordinary roster recognition does not use a remote AI or analytics service.

These /crewscheduler/ pages provide static product information. It has no support upload form, advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies set by us. Hosting and network providers may process standard connection data, such as IP addresses, browser information, request paths, and timestamps, to deliver pages, operate the service, and maintain security. Local processing in the app does not mean this website has no connection logs.

We limit data use and access and avoid unnecessary collection. Temporary diagnostic files use iOS file protection. Device, email, and backup security also depend on your device lock and chosen services. No transmission or storage method is absolutely secure.

6. Your choices and policy changes

You can decline to select images, skip reports, disable attachments, or cancel sending. Stopping future sharing does not retract content already sent. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of support information held by the developer. We may verify the original sender address or report details to avoid disclosing your data to another person.

The app is intended for crew roster organization and is not directed at children. Do not submit unnecessary information about children or third parties.

We will update this page and its date when features or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the website or app as appropriate, and consent will be requested for new purposes where legally required. Contact us if the Traditional Chinese and English versions appear inconsistent.
