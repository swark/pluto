寄送客服郵件 ↗閱讀隱私政策

請勿提供公司登入密碼、驗證碼或未經授權的他人班表。若需要附圖，請先遮蔽與問題無關的姓名、員工編號及其他個人資訊。客服網頁不接受直接上傳檔案。

開始使用

  1. 選取清晰、格式受支援的華航機組員班表截圖；多張圖片請依班表順序選取。
  2. 保持年月、日期與勤務資訊完整可見，開始辨識，必要時確認月份。
  3. 在月曆逐日檢查辨識結果，修正航段、輪值與個人標記。
  4. 需要保存或交換資料時，使用設定中的「匯出或備份班表」；開啟匯出選單後選擇 JSON 備份或班表圖片。

目前 App 要求 iOS 18.2 以上；只支援目前識別的特定班表格式，並非所有航空公司或所有截圖版型。

常見問題

辨識錯誤或沒有出現完整班表，怎麼辦？

使用原始、清晰的截圖，避免過度裁切、模糊或縮小圖片。確認年月、日期與勤務欄位可見；多張截圖請依序選取，並核對偵測的月份。仍有問題時，可在 App 設定點選「回報辨識問題」，或寄信提供重現步驟。

如何安全地寄送診斷回報？

在 App 設定開啟「回報辨識問題」，填寫說明，逐項檢查附件開關與「將送出的資料」。附件可能依問題類型預選；App 不會自動遮蔽姓名或員工編號。只寄送重現問題必需的內容，並在寄送或分享前確認收件人。

App 說無法寄送郵件，怎麼辦？

App 內寄信需要裝置的 Mail 能寄送郵件。你可以設定 Mail 帳號，或使用「分享回報」將 ZIP 交給其他郵件 App，再寄到此頁的客服信箱。也可直接用你常用的信箱聯絡。

關閉 App 後，班表會保留嗎？

App 會在本機儲存最後成功轉換或匯入的班表，並保留編輯與標記供重開還原。這不是雲端同步；換機或刪除 App 前，請先匯出 JSON 備份，並妥善保存。

如何還原備份？

在 App 的班表匯入功能選取先前匯出的 JSON 檔案，依畫面指示完成匯入，再檢查月份與班表。圖片分享僅供檢視，無法代替保留班表結構的 JSON 備份。

如何刪除 App 資料？

到 iOS「設定 → 一般 → iPhone／iPad 儲存空間 → CrewScheduler → 刪除 App」。請選「刪除 App」而非會保留資料的「卸載 App」。匯出的檔案、照片、系統備份與已寄送的回報副本，需要另外管理；詳見隱私政策。

這是華航官方 App 嗎？可以用來確認勤務嗎？

CrewScheduler 是獨立開發的班表整理工具，非中華航空官方產品，也不代表公司發布勤務資訊。OCR 與內建時刻表可能不完整或過期；請以航空公司正式班表與通知為準。

Support

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Contact the developer

Product support and privacy contact: CrewScheduler developer (Swark).

swark.yang@swarkdoit.com

We support English and Traditional Chinese. Include your app version, iOS version, device model, steps to reproduce the issue, and expected versus actual behavior. Replies are by email; this is not a live or emergency duty-support channel.

Do not send airline passwords, verification codes, or another person’s roster without permission. Remove unrelated names, employee IDs, and other personal information before attaching screenshots. This website has no file-upload form.

Getting started

  1. Select clear screenshots of a supported China Airlines crew roster. Choose multiple images in roster order.
  2. Keep month, dates, and duty details visible. Start recognition and confirm the month if prompted.
  3. Review each day in the calendar and correct flight legs, rotations, and personal markers.
  4. Use “Export or back up roster” in Settings to open the export menu and save a JSON backup or share a calendar image.

The current app requires iOS 18.2 or later. Only the currently recognized roster format is supported; not every airline or screenshot layout works.

Recognition problems

Try original, clear screenshots without excessive cropping or resizing. Keep month, dates, and duty columns visible, choose images in order, and verify the detected month. If the issue persists, use “Report a recognition issue” in Settings or email the reproduction steps.

Sending a diagnostic report

Describe the issue, review every attachment toggle and the “Data to Send” list, and check the recipient. Some attachments are preselected depending on issue type. Names and employee IDs are not automatically redacted. Include only what is necessary to reproduce the problem.

Mail is unavailable

In-app email needs a working Mail configuration on your device. Configure Mail, use “Share Report” to hand the ZIP to another email app, or contact the support address directly using your preferred email service.

Saving and restoring a roster

The last successfully converted or imported roster and local edits are stored on your device for relaunch restoration. There is no app-provided cloud sync. Export a JSON backup before changing devices or deleting the app. Use the app’s roster import feature to select a previously exported JSON, then review its month and contents. A shared image is not a structured JSON backup.

Deleting data

Use iOS Settings → General → iPhone / iPad Storage → CrewScheduler → Delete App. Offload App retains data. Manage exported files, source photos, backups, and already-shared reports separately.

Official duty information

CrewScheduler is independently developed and is not an official China Airlines product. OCR results and the bundled timetable may be incomplete or outdated. Always follow your airline’s official roster and notices.

Privacy requests

Email swark.yang@swarkdoit.com to request access, correction, or deletion of support information received by the developer. Provide the original sender address, date, or report ID and describe your request. Do not send identity documents or unnecessary sensitive data. Read the full privacy policy for details.
